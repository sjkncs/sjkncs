<table>

#### 🇬🇧 Exploring the Boundaries

I'm deeply fascinated by the intersection of **Ecology, Environmental Science, and Artificial Intelligence**. My current explorations include:

- 🌍 **Species Distribution Modeling** — Using deep learning to predict how species respond to climate change
- 🛰️ **Remote Sensing + CV** — Applying computer vision to satellite imagery for land cover classification and deforestation detection
- 🌊 **Environmental Time-Series Forecasting** — Leveraging LSTMs and Transformers for water quality, air pollution, and climate predictions
- 🧬 **eDNA & Biodiversity Analysis** — Combining bioinformatics with NLP techniques for environmental DNA metabarcoding
- 🌱 **Ecosystem Simulation** — Using agent-based models and optimization to simulate ecological dynamics
- 📊 **Conservation AI** — Building intelligent tools for wildlife monitoring and habitat preservation

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

我对 **生态学、环境科学与人工智能** 的交叉领域深感着迷。当前的探索方向包括：

- 🌍 **物种分布建模** — 利用深度学习预测物种对气候变化的响应

- 🌊 **环境时间序列预测** — 借助LSTM和Transformer预测水质、空气污染与气候变化
