The overall direction is a project-first engineering and research profile, not an imitation of any one README. Card descriptions are manually curated from the user's public repositories. Stars, primary language and push dates are actual repository metadata, refreshed by the existing daily workflow. Stars and non-fork status do not prove originality, project quality or scientific validity.
